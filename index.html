How to use a lesson page

  1. Open the lesson and press Full screen.
  2. Work through the steps on the left. Each step has its own timer.
  3. Press Teacher notes to see tips. Keep them off while students watch.
  4. Audio uses the computer's voices. Chrome or Edge usually sound best.
